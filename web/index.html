Method and citation

        
Relaxations are run with vasp-mace, an open-source (MIT licence) VASP-style interface to MACE machine-learning interatomic potentials, using the MACE-MH-1 multi-head foundation model by default (MACE-MP-0 is also available). If you use this section in your work, please cite:

        
    
          
  • vasp-mace: Grau-Crespo, R. vasp-mace: a VASP-style workflow interface for MACE machine-learning interatomic potentials (2025). Zenodo. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19479246
    • 
          
  • MACE: Batatia, I.; Kovács, D. P.; Simm, G. N. C.; Ortner, C.; Csányi, G. “MACE: Higher Order Equivariant Message Passing Neural Networks for Fast and Accurate Force Fields”. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS), 2022.
    • 
          
  • MACE-MH-1 foundation model: Batatia, I. et al. “Cross Learning between Electronic Structure Theories for Unifying Molecular, Surface, and Inorganic Crystal Foundation Force Fields.” arXiv:2510.25380 (2025). arxiv.org/abs/2510.25380. The model is released under the ASL (Academic Software License); check its terms before commercial use.
    • 
          
  • MACE-MP-0 foundation model: Batatia, I. et al. “A foundation model for atomistic materials chemistry.” The Journal of Chemical Physics 163, no. 18 (2025).
